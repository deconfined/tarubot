TaruBot v2 was shaped by the people who used it first. Thank you to everyone below.

## Testers

These people tested TaruBot v2 on the test server, before launch and after it. They're listed by the names they use on Discord.

- **PigeonMuffin** tested both sides of officer access: as an officer through their in-game rank, and as a regular member while that access was revoked, before it was restored and handed back to the rank. They saw the replies a member gets for commands that aren't theirs to use, and tried to apply as a guest while applications were closed. When a link an officer had made for them was removed and they linked the character again themselves, they found the bot left them without a main character; a new link now becomes the main when a member has no other.
- **pazzberry** went through guest access as a visitor: applying as a guest, and having Guest granted and revoked.
- **moonbun_art** and **poppymori** each linked a character for the first time, checking verification, the main character and the nickname that follows it. moonbun_art also tried the gil ledger as a member: a deposit, the balance and the history.

Between them, they saw the character links, the officer and member views, guest applications, the ledger and the bot's replies the way the people using them do.

## Built on

TaruBot reads the Lodestone with the community-maintained [`xivapi/lodestone-css-selectors`](https://github.com/xivapi/lodestone-css-selectors) definitions, which its parser applies and keeps current. Thank you to everyone who maintains them. The selector data keeps its own license.

The dashboard and this site are set in [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif), [Manrope](https://github.com/sharanda/manrope) and [JetBrains Mono](https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono), under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The dashboard's icons are from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), under the ISC License. Their copyright notices and license texts are in the [third-party licenses](/tarubot/third-party-licenses.txt) file.
